# Heparin Dose Calculator 🩺

A clinical medical tool designed to calculate Heparin dosages (initial bolus, infusion rates, and dosage adjustments based on PTT lab results) accurately and rapidly, adhering to standard medical protocols to minimize bleeding risks and ensure effective anticoagulation therapy.

---

## Features ✨

* **Accurate & Rapid Calculations:** Eliminates manual calculation errors in high-pressure clinical environments.
* **Protocol-Based Adjustments:** Follows standard guidelines for continuous intravenous (IV) Heparin infusions and titrations.
* **Safety First:** Built-in checks and clear recommendations for healthcare professionals.

---

## Target Audience 👥

Designed for healthcare providers, including:
* Physicians
* Pharmacists
* Nurses

---

## Disclaimer ⚠️

> **Medical Disclaimer:** This tool is intended for educational and professional healthcare use only. It should not replace clinical judgment or institutional protocols. Always verify calculations before administering medication.
